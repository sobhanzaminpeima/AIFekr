# Template 3 — Showcase your app (phone mockup / green screen)

This tool replaces the green screen on a real phone photo with your screenshot. For each mockup, upload one green-screen phone photo plus one screenshot of the system.

## Screenshots to prepare (mobile/responsive)

Before capturing, open the browser in mobile view (e.g. 375×812, like an iPhone) so the UI renders responsively, then take the screenshot:

| # | Screen | Why it matters |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Chat screen (message + AI reply) | Core product intro — the first thing users see |
| 2 | Image generation result | Most visually striking scene for mobile |
| 3 | Generated video clip/preview | Shows the platform is multimedia |
| 4 | CRM dashboard (leads list or pipeline) | For business/sales audience |
| 5 | Business dashboard (Business Doctor / summary report) | Shows the platform's analytical intelligence |
| 6 | SEO agents screen | For marketing/SEO audience |

## Technical notes
- Choose a vertical phone mockup, since most of this product's mobile UI is vertical.
- High contrast and readable text matter — before screenshotting, make sure sample data is showing (not an empty state/loading spinner).
- Upload each scene as a separate run; in final editing, stitch these 6 scenes together (2-3 seconds each) with music and voiceover on top.
