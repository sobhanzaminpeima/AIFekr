# AI course generation and existing credits

Course generation uses `AI_COURSE_GENERATION` in the existing SiteSetting-backed Credit Rules, default 100. The administrator sees the dynamic price before starting; a changed server price requires reconfirmation. The existing guarded User/Team debit, AI-wallet mirror and UsageLog remain authoritative. No separate credit balance or financial ledger was introduced.

One durable job reserves the entire cost before any provider request. Idempotency is scoped to actor and request key, with a request fingerprint and per-course generation lock. All outline/lesson stages share this reservation. A validated course and committed ledger state are saved atomically. Invalid output, provider failure, timeout or failed persistence refunds the original wallet once. The payer snapshot also restores exactly the mirrored AI-wallet amount after team membership changes. Expired leases are reconciled by the authenticated watchdog and admin reads; late worker results cannot commit after refund.

Lifecycle: DRAFT → GENERATING → GENERATED (review required) → PUBLISHED → ARCHIVED. Only published courses appear to eligible active students. Failed regeneration preserves the earlier course. Manual edits are free; entire-course regeneration requires a separate explicit cost confirmation. Granular paid regeneration actions were not introduced. Existing admin logs show AI Course Generation, title, status and signed credit delta.

Learning, normal quiz grading, lesson/course completion and already-earned certificate downloads are deterministic, authenticated operations with no AI or credit call. Quiz keys remain server-side until a submitted attempt. Certificates are scoped to the learner, issued once for the completed version and retain the earned name/title snapshot; they record course completion rather than accreditation.

Validation: full suite 122 files / 864 tests passed, including isolated bank and voice integrations. The new course tests cover 150 → 50; refusal at 99; provider/empty/invalid output refunds; duplicate completed/in-progress requests; mismatched idempotency keys; expired lease recovery and late-result fencing; failed commit; configurable price changes; confirmed regeneration; manual edits; student authorization; published-only catalog; zero-charge learning/completion/downloads; original-team refunds. Final targeted run: 34 tests passed. TypeScript and changed-file lint passed. Production build passed.

Actual local browser scenario: React course generated with real providers, six chapters and a final project; shared credit balance changed 2500 → 2400 once. The preceding network-denied attempt returned all credits. Manual overview editing left the balance at 2400. Cost and committed/refunded history are visible in local screenshot course-generation-admin-proof.png. A browser-native confirmation stalled subsequent click automation; it was replaced with an application dialog, but the revised dialog's final click interaction is not claimed as verified. Publishing/learning authorization and zero-cost completion were verified by integration tests.

All synthetic accounts, generated QA course content, local databases and screenshots are excluded from deployment. Passing tests does not certify AI educational accuracy; administrators must review draft content before publication.
